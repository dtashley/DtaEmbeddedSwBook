Insektengericht

This chapter is named after a German-language Beavis & Butthead comic book I purchased at the Munich train station in the 1990s.

A page or two of the book was titled Insektengericht (insect court).  Beavis & Butthead judge insects to be guilty of various crimes and impose judgment.  A typical crime was Käfersein, the verdict was always schuldig, and a typical sentence was Tod durch Explodieren (translations: being a beetle, guilty, and death by explosion).

In a similar sense, in good humor, in this chapter I sit in judgment of all the bugs (and other product problems) I've encountered in my career.
