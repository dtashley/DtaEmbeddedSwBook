Preface

The book contains my notes on various topics from 25+ years or developing embedded software.

I don't want to compare this book to Brooks' classic The Mythical Man-Month, as Brooks was a better software developer / software architect / manager than me.  Still, the flavor may be similar.

I've prepared this book in ePub format, to minimize the waste of paper and so that a true übernerd can view this on a variety of devices, including cellphones.

This book is distributed through Amazon for $4.99.  I plan to release a new edition about once a year.

I hope this book contributes to your self-actualization as a software engineer.

Dave Ashley
